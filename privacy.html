Olympus Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Olympus is a private, personal assistant app used only by its owner. No one else can sign in to it or use it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Olympus reads and manages events on the owner's own Google Calendar, including calendars shared with the owner. It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

Calendar data is used only to schedule, remind, and plan for the owner. It is processed on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared with, or made available to anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Olympus's access at any time at myaccount.google.com, under Security, Third-party access.
